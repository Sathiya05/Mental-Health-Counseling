Choosing well

How to choose your therapist

Fit matters more than credentials alone. Three unhurried steps — and you can change direction at any point.

  1. 01

    Start with your concern

    Use the filters above — anxiety, couples, family, growth — and shortlist two or three profiles that feel close to your situation.

  2. 02

    Book one first session

    Treat it as a mutual introduction. Notice whether you feel heard, unrushed and clear about what happens next.

  3. 03

    Switch freely if needed

    No explanation owed. Tell us or ask for a match and we'll move you to someone who fits better.
