Who we are

A small team that stays small on purpose

Serenity is an independent counseling practice with eighteen therapists across individual therapy, couples work, family counseling and personal growth. We are not a hospital, we are not a clinic queue — we are a quiet place with good chairs, unhurried sessions and people who pay attention.

Every therapist here holds recognised qualifications, takes part in regular supervision, and is trained to work within their scope. Where something sits beyond that scope, we say so and help you find the right person rather than guessing.

Our philosophy

Safety first. Honesty always. Progress at your pace.

Three ideas shape every session we run. They're simple, and they cost us something to keep.

  • 1

    Safety before insight

    You cannot work on something difficult while feeling exposed. We earn trust before we ask for anything.

  • 2

    Honest, gentle feedback

    We will tell you what we see, kindly and directly. Agreement for its own sake helps no one.

  • 3

    Your pace, your call

    You decide how deep to go, how often to come, and when you are done. Nothing is decided for you.

Our approach

What actually happens in a session

No mystery, no jargon. Here is the shape of a typical 50-minute session.

  1. 01

    Arrive

    A few minutes to settle, and a check-in on how the week has been.

  2. 02

    Explore

    We look at what is happening, and what sits underneath it.

  3. 03

    Practise

    You try something new in the room, before it has to work outside it.

  4. 04

    Reflect

    A couple of minutes to note what mattered, and what comes next.

Safe & confidential

Built to feel like a place you can breathe in

Our rooms are private, sound-treated and never shared. Notes are kept securely, conversations are never discussed between therapists without your consent, and you can ask what is on your file at any time.

  • Private entrance and reception-free arrival
  • Clear confidentiality agreement at the first session
  • Accessible rooms, seating options and quiet space
  • Request a companion or support person at any time

If you need urgent help: Serenity is not an emergency service. Contact your local emergency number or a 24/7 crisis helpline in your country. Messages sent through this website are not monitored for emergencies.
