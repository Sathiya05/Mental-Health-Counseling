Service 01

Individual Therapy

One-to-one counseling for whatever you are carrying — anxiety, low mood, burnout, difficult relationships, or simply a sense that something has quietly shifted and you would like it to shift back.

Who it may support

  • Feeling stuck, anxious or unmotivated
  • Work or study pressure and burnout
  • Low self-worth or self-compassion

What sessions may involve

  • Untangling recurring patterns
  • Practical tools for difficult days
  • Reviewing progress every few weeks

Service 02

Couples Therapy

A structured, balanced space for two people who want to understand each other better — whether you are in crisis, considering a change, or simply stuck in the same argument.

Who it may support

  • Recurring conflict or communication breakdown
  • Rebuilding trust after distance
  • Navigating a major life change together

What sessions may involve

  • New ways of listening and responding
  • Untangling expectations and unspoken needs
  • Clearer decisions, together

Your counselor stays neutral — the goal is mutual understanding, not winning.

Service 03

Anxiety & Stress Support

Support for the everyday kind — the racing thoughts before sleep, the tightness in the morning, the feeling of always being slightly behind. Learn what is happening and what helps.

Who it may support

  • Constant worry and busy mind
  • Overwhelm and burnout
  • Difficulty switching off

What sessions may involve

  • Understanding your body's stress signals
  • Grounding and pacing techniques
  • Building sustainable routines

Service 04

Personal Growth

Not everything that hurts needs fixing. Sometimes you simply want to understand yourself better, build confidence, and change habits that no longer serve you.

Who it may support

  • Low confidence and self-doubt
  • Habits you would like to change
  • Building resilience for the future

What sessions may involve

  • Values and long-term direction
  • Replacing harsh self-talk
  • Small, repeatable experiments

Service 05

Family Counseling

For households navigating tension, change or distance. We work with parents, couples and adult children together — or in carefully planned smaller combinations.

Who it may support

  • Teens and parents in conflict
  • Blended families and step-parenting
  • Caring for an ageing parent

What sessions may involve

  • Understanding the pattern, not just the argument
  • Agreeing on how to talk at home
  • Individual sessions alongside family work
