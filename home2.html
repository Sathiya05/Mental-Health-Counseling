The approach

Care that starts with listening

Before any technique or plan, there's conversation. We take time to understand how you arrived here, what you're carrying and what a good outcome would look like for you.

Session structure

Weekly 50-minute sessions, with a short check-in at the start and time to reflect at the end.

Written summaries

Optional session notes you can read later, so progress is visible and never a surprise.

In person or online

Choose a quiet room at the center, or a secure video call from wherever you are.

Review together

Every few sessions we pause and check what's working, and what to adjust.

1:1

Every session is yours alone. No groups, no waiting rooms.

What the first month feels like

A rough sketch of how counseling unfolds — so nothing feels like a leap of faith.

  1. Week 1

    Conversation

    We talk about what brought you in and what you want to feel different.

  2. Week 2–3

    Patterns

    Together we notice what's really going on beneath the surface.

  3. Week 4–6

    Practice

    Small, repeatable tools you can use between sessions.

  4. Ongoing

    Momentum

    Review what's working, adjust what isn't, and keep going at your pace.
